About LinTranscend Tech

凌越・Lin 科技成立于2020年，是一家专注于智能计算领域的高科技企业。公司汇聚了来自全球顶尖高校和科技企业的精英人才，致力于通过技术创新推动智能时代的发展。

Founded in 2020, LinTranscend Tech is a high-tech enterprise focused on the field of intelligent computing. The company brings together elite talents from top universities and technology companies around the world, dedicated to driving the development of the intelligent era through technological innovation.

我们秉承"创新、卓越、协作、诚信"的核心价值观，以"Lin 启智算，凌越科技新境"为使命，不断突破技术边界，为客户创造更大价值。

We adhere to the core values of "Innovation, Excellence, Collaboration, Integrity" and take "Lin Ignites Intelligence, Transcend Tech Frontiers" as our mission, constantly breaking through technical boundaries to create greater value for customers.
